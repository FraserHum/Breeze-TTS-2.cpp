# BREEZE-DEPTH-NEXT results

## Decision and next step

The bounded four-utterance pilot is complete. It does not produce a deployment-ready compression candidate. Keeping 75% of contiguous post-SwiGLU blocks leaves held-out down-output relative MSE of `0.0851`, `0.191`, and `0.0887` for layers 0, 5, and 11. Rounded F16 rank 64 uses `1,179,648` bytes versus `4,718,592` Q4_K bytes, with held-out relative MSE `0.518`, `0.839`, and `0.380`. These are tensor-level screens against the dequantized dense down reference. They do not disprove broader sparsity or low-rank methods; they rule out treating these simple pilot configurations as deployment-ready.

The four verified unfused one-branch captures passed the full shape/blob validator; the final corpus receipt records baseline code and WAV equality for each pair. The screen revalidates every input before reading it. Calibration and evaluation remain disjoint English/Mandarin pairs with 96 calibration rows per layer. That limits the mathematical regression support to rank 95 and leaves no reference-voice or long-utterance coverage.

## Exact FFN probe outcome

The final same-binary row-specialization experiment used the existing integer Q4_K MMVQ path, with no custom shader retained. The receipt is `benchmarks/depth-corpus/row-specialization.json`. Three repeats of each variant produced identical baseline WAV hashes and 213 frames:

| variant | mean wall RTF | mean depth time/frame |
|---|---:|---:|
| one-row control | 0.942852 | 51.35 ms |
| two-row | 0.969329 | 53.02 ms |
| four-row | 0.969560 | 53.51 ms |

Both multi-row variants are rejected. Their depth-stage penalties are about 1.67 and 2.16 ms/frame; the corresponding whole-run wall penalties are about 2.118 and 2.137 ms/frame from the mean-RTF differences. The one-row result is approximately the existing `0.95` root baseline; this experiment does not establish that an RTF target has been reached. The earlier native large-workgroup path is also rejected. Those results close the two low-risk exact-FFN probes; they do not exhaust custom kernel designs, so no further bespoke kernel is prioritized from this pilot.

- Keeping 75% of contiguous post-SwiGLU blocks leaves held-out output rel MSE of layer 0 0.0851, layer 5 0.191, layer 11 0.0887; the logical aligned read is 3,538,944 down bytes per call (10,616,832 for gate/up/down), not a resident-storage claim.

No compression deployment follows from this report.
